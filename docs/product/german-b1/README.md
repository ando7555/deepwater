# German B1 practice exam

Owner ask, 2026-11-17: "in the same place do German similar" (like the Danish PD3 practice). Not merged until the owner says so.

## Which exam, and why

A German B1 certificate exam, in the shape of the **Goethe-Zertifikat B1**. B1 is the German level asked for naturalisation
in Germany (§10 StAG, as summarised by city information sheets such as Karlsruhe's and Heilbronn's), and B1 certificates
from Goethe-Institut and telc are the ones those sheets list. The Goethe B1 has four modules that can be taken separately:
Lesen (5 parts, 65 min), Hören (4 parts, about 40 min), Schreiben (3 parts, 60 min) and Sprechen (in pairs, about 15 min),
each scored out of 100 (goethe.de B1 pages and model test). telc Deutsch B1 and the DTZ (integration course test, B1 result)
are similar. Which level an employer wants is not verified here; many jobs ask for B2.

## What the learner sees

Echo, German: `Course | B1 exam practice`, the same switch as Danish. Four parts in the Goethe order:

- Reading (Lesen): a job advert and a short article, 7 questions.
- Listening (Hören): 5 recordings read by the browser's German voice, two plays each.
- Writing (Schreiben): an informal email (70-100 words), a forum opinion (70-100) and a short polite message (35-60),
  like Goethe Schreiben parts 1-3.
- Speaking (Sprechen): plan something together, present a topic, and react to a partner's talk with a question. The real
  exam is in pairs; here the learner says their part. German speech recognition (de-DE) where the browser has it.

Items are Deepwater's own (`backend/src/main/resources/exam/german-b1.json`), labelled "not official". Same pass rule and
examiner as the other exams; the fixed rubric uses German letters (ä, ö, ü, ß), linking words and common words.
