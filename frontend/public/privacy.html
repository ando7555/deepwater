Privacy and contact

Applies to the Deepwater website and the Deepwater iPhone app: Echo, Harbor and Amber. Last updated 15 November 2026.
Contact us

Questions, privacy requests, or a safety concern about another Amber member: email CONTACT_EMAIL_TO_BE_SET. A person reads every safety report.

What we collect and why

  • Your account: name, email address and a password hash (we never store the password itself), or your Google account's name and email if you sign in with Google. Used to sign you in and to send account emails such as email confirmation.
  • Your profile (optional): phone number, city, country, native language and a short intro, if you add them. Only you can see them.
  • Echo: your learning goal and level, your answers, cards, practice history and Polish B1 practice attempts. Used to choose what you practise next and to show your progress.
  • Harbor: the CV you upload (file name and text), the talent profile built from it, your job preferences and your application tracker. Used to match you with jobs. Only you can see them.
  • Amber (adults only): the first name, age, area, interests and intro you choose to show, your choices (pass, hide, invite), your conversations, and blocks and reports. Other members see only what you put on your Amber profile. Echo data is shared with Amber only if you switch on "learning Danish".

Who else processes your data

  • Railway hosts our servers and database.
  • Postmark sends account emails (your email address and the message).
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Anthropic (AI examiner): your Polish B1 writing and speaking answers are sent to Anthropic's Claude for marking only if you tick the permission box when you hand in. Without it, fixed rules mark them and nothing is sent.
  • Anthropic (Ask Deepwater assistant): when you use the assistant, your questions and the Deepwater data it looks up to answer them (for example your cards, exam results or job matches) are sent to Anthropic's Claude, only after you tick the permission box in the assistant panel. You can withdraw that permission in the panel at any time. It can read your own Amber profile if you ask about it, but never other members' profiles or any messages.
  • Speech recognition in speaking practice uses your browser's or phone's speech service (on iPhone, Apple's).

We require these providers to protect your data at least as well as this policy does. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Amber safety

Amber is for people aged 18 or older. Profiles and messages are checked against a list of words Amber does not allow. You can block or report anyone at any time; a report blocks the person straight away and is reviewed by a person, who can remove them from Amber. The reported person is never told who reported them.

How long we keep data, and deleting it

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • Delete account on your profile page deletes your account and everything above, including Amber conversations, straight away. Only a random id stays in our audit log to record that a deletion happened. Reports other members filed about you stay for review.
  • Leave Amber deletes your Amber profile, invitations and messages; your Deepwater account stays.
  • You can withdraw permission for the AI examiner at any time by leaving the box unticked.
  • To get a copy of your data or ask anything else, email us at the address above.
